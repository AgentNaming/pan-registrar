# Security

Please report a vulnerability privately, through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" on this repository's
Security tab, not in a public issue. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version.

We answer within three working days, and say when a fix is expected. A fix ships as a patch release,
with credit to the reporter unless they would rather not be named.

The parts that matter most are the ones a registrar's trust rests on: card signatures and canonical JSON
(SPEC-NAMING §5.3), pairing (§4.1), re-homing and referrals (§5.6, §7.8), the witnessing and moderation
credentials (§3.1, §9), and the history log (§6).
